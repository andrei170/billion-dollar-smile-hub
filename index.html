Compliance Notes

+

The build is engineered around the compliance edges of a cosmetic-dental brand with a non-US-licensed founder, a small review base, and a celebrity-press surface.

  • US licensure framing. Every page frames the work as designed by Dr. Mario Montoya, delivered by our US-licensed Atlanta team (Dr. Tomei, Dr. Herrera, Dr. Caceres). The AJC piece flagged the licensure question and the site answers it on the page, before they Google it.
  • No pricing lifted verbatim. The press-reported figures ($25K full set, $4,400 implants) appear nowhere. All pricing language is "consult-based" or "investment varies by case."
  • Typographic only. No celebrity photos (Rick Ross, T-Pain, Ari Fletcher) reused. No first-party clinical photos hotlinked. Before-and-after gallery is shown privately in consult, framed for patient-privacy reasons.
  • Honest reviews scale. The reviews section shows the verified 23-review base today and pairs it with an honest practice note. The acquisition engine closes the gap month over month.
  • Domain consolidation. One canonical US site. 301 the rest. Robots-block the staging subdomain. IG bio link re-pointed.
  • Review-acquisition compliance. No gating, no incentives, no fabricated reviews. Compliant with Google review policy and US FTC review-gating rules.
